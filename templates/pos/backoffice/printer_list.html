Connecting a till to its printer and drawer

  1. Plug the cash drawer into the printer's drawer port (RJ11), and the printer into the shop network. Print the printer's self-test page to see its IP address.
  2. On the till PC, run the Mofisi bridge (in the project's tools/mofisi-bridge folder): python mofisi_bridge.py --origin {{ request.scheme }}://{{ request.get_host }}. It listens on http://127.0.0.1:9180, talks only to printers on the shop LAN, and keeps working offline.
  3. Add the printer here with its IP and port 9100, then on that till PC press Use on this device, Test print and Test drawer.

Epson ePOS and Star WebPRNT printers can also be driven straight from the browser (a site on HTTPS needs the printer's own HTTPS, e.g. Epson on port 8043, or the bridge). If Mofisi runs on a computer in the shop, choose "the server itself can reach" and no bridge is needed.
