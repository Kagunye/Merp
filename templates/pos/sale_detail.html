A refund is its own ticket rather than an edit of the sale: the money that went over the counter and the money that comes back are two events, and the till has to be able to prove both. The goods go back on {{ sale.warehouse.name|default:"the till's shelf" }} at the same cost they left at, and the revenue and VAT are taken back by a second entry — never by deleting this one. The money goes back by {{ sale.get_payment_method_display }}, the way it came in{% if sale.payment_method == 'CASH' %}, out of your own drawer at {{ refund_till.branch.name }} — so it has to hold enough notes{% endif %}.
