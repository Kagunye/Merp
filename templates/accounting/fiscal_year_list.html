Closing a period empties its revenue, cost and expense balances into Current Year Profit (3300) with a posted closing entry, and year end sweeps that into Retained Earnings (3200). Reopening reverses the entry instead of editing history, so the audit trail keeps both sides. Bookings dated inside a closed period are still allowed but flagged on the entry.
