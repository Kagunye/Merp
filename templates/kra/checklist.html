About this checklist

KRA does not publish its certification test sheet: it hands it out during vetting (ask timsupport@kra.go.ke). This checklist follows the function groups and ordering rules of KRA's OSCU/VSCU v2.0 specifications: initialisation, code sync, customers, items, imports, sales and credit notes, purchases, stock, and the offline queue.

Simulator run: plays every scenario against the built-in KRA simulator inside a transaction that is rolled back. Your devices, numbers and queue are untouched and nothing is sent anywhere.

Sandbox run: plays the same scenarios against your configured backend in KRA's sandbox. Offered only when the environment is Sandbox and the backend is not the simulator.
